## gurbaninow date parse on the SGPC timestamp

gurbaninow reads the first `.customDate` matching `\d{1,2}-\d{1,2}-\d{4}`, trims it, and passes it to date-fns `parse(text, 'dd-MM-yyyy')`. date-fns returns Invalid Date when non-whitespace remains after the pattern, and index.js then throws on `toISOString()`, falling back to SikhNet. Archive responses carry no timestamp, so this is counted over index.php captures only; archive days are N/A. The throw follows from reading gurbaninow lib/sgpc.js and index.js; date-fns is not installed in that clone, so it was not executed. The capture itself is checked byte by byte below.

## Source quirks

- The archive endpoint is `hssearchhukmanamaTestCalender.php` (POST `selected_date=YYYY-MM-DD`), so its "Test" name is part of the contract.
- The English ang label varies between `(Page: N)` and `(Ang: N)`.
- The index.php timestamp has a double space before the time (`DD-MM-YYYY  HH:MM AM`).
- The timestamp is the reading time, not the posting time; the page keeps the previous day until SGPC posts.
- A day with nothing published returns HTTP 200 with `supported:false`.
- No cookie is set or needed.

## Known issues carried over from Hazur Sahib, deliberately unfixed

- `stripAngSuffix` builds its regex from a template literal containing `\s`, which becomes a literal `s`. It still strips an ang suffix written without spaces; the SGPC path passes the ang through `opts.ang` and never relies on it.
- `extractCounters` misses chained counters: counter 4 followed directly by counter 2, sharing the middle double danda, yields `[4]`, not `[4, 2]`.
